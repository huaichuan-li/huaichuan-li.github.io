多模态大模型Multimodal LLM

大创项目「基于大模型的跨模态理解与预测研究」（负责人 / 第一作者）：提出 SomnoVLM 睡眠分期框架，将 EEG 波形图像与频谱证据融合建模，仅训练极少参数即超越现有 VLM 方案，并生成可审计的解释报告；成果投稿至国际学术会议。Innovation project "Cross-modal Understanding and Prediction with Large Models" (Lead / First Author): proposed SomnoVLM, a sleep staging framework fusing EEG waveform images with spectral evidence — surpassing existing VLM baselines with minimal trainable parameters and auditable explanations; submitted to an international academic conference.
